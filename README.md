# AI-Enabled Strategic Decision Intelligence

## Research Title

The Impact of Data Quality on AI-Enabled Strategic Decision Intelligence: An Experimental Analysis

## Research Area

Artificial Intelligence and Autonomy

## Course

Data Analytics and Strategic Decision Intelligence

## Research Question

How does data quality degradation affect the predictive performance and reliability of AI-enabled strategic decision-making?

## Research Objectives

1. Evaluate machine-learning model performance using clean business data.
2. Measure the effect of controlled data-quality degradation on AI-based decision performance.
3. Compare the robustness of different machine-learning models under different data-quality conditions.

## Models

- Logistic Regression
- Random Forest
- XGBoost

## Evaluation Metrics

- Accuracy
- Precision
- Recall
- F1-score
- ROC-AUC

## Experimental Conditions

The experiment will compare model performance under clean data and controlled levels of data-quality degradation.
